# Feedback frequency matters more than feedback quality in skill acquisition

When feedback arrives weeks after an attempt, the learner keeps repeating the faulty movement in the meantime, and the error hardens into habit. Fast feedback shortens the try-adjust loop so mistakes are corrected before they become automatic.
